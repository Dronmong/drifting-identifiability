A repaired drifting model, six times sharper

Both models below are encoder-free and generate an image in a single forward pass. They share an architecture, a dataset and a 650,000-update budget. The only differences are a numerical candidate and a two-line repair to the training objective — found by measuring why the original could not train.

The mechanism

Direct-x Euler Mean Flow in raw pixels: the network predicts a denoised image from a noised one and a time pair, and inference calls it exactly once. No latent space, no encoder, no decoder, no sampler loop. The forward count is asserted by a module hook during evaluation rather than assumed — 100 calls produced the 50,000 images behind every FID on this page.

What was actually wrong

The campaign began by chasing a finite-difference conditioning failure. That failure was real, and fixing it was not what mattered.

Probing the preserved base checkpoint with a reproduction of one production optimizer update — same weights, same embedding scale, changing only which (t, r) pairs were drawn — moved the median gradient norm from 2.46 to 1066.78. A 434× swing from the sampler alone.

The successor sampler draws far more low-t rows than its predecessor, and the objective's 1/t² weight — clamped at 0.02, so a row at t = 0.01 outweighs one at t = 1 by 2500× — converted that into gradients three to five orders of magnitude above the clip. Every single update clipped. The learning-rate schedule was decorative.

The repair is two constants: weight the time axis uniformly, and set the clip to the measured 95th percentile of the gradient it actually has to bound. Clipping fell from 100% of updates to 4.1%, inside the protocol's own 5% allowance.

None of this was visible before because the check that should have caught it was reporting a fabricated 0.0 instead of a measurement. The base model's real clip rate was 15.3% against the same 5% limit.

Read this before quoting the number

Four caveats that belong beside 18.92

  • Admission thresholds were relaxed after seeing the data, deliberately and on authorization. An audit confirmed the relaxation still rejects both the documented ten-radian control (2/63 rows) and the originally failing candidate, and that the threshold doing the discriminating was left untouched — but admission is no longer an independent check at its original strength.
  • The configuration rests on a single seed per arm. The A/B behind the repair is n = 1, against a project history of FID seed deviation near 27. The clip fraction moving 100% → 4.1% is mechanical and not a seed effect; the FID magnitude wants replication.
  • This is a capability run, not a gated foundation. The sealed pipeline binds its admission subject, baseline evaluation and forensics to one checkpoint, which an off-scale candidate cannot satisfy. Forcing it would have meant rewriting that provenance chain, so it was not forced.
  • Not state of the art. Strong multi-step CIFAR-10 diffusion reaches FID 3–10 and StyleGAN2-ADA about 2.9. The claim here is narrower and specific: a one-step, encoder-free model beating its own fully trained predecessor by 4.4×.
